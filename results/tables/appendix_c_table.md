# Table C1. Text Effects under Full-Sample vs. Fold-Internal Preprocessing

Re-implemented pipeline from raw narrative texts (Okt morphological analysis, content POS,
negation/emphasis tagging, stopwords, document-frequency filter, TF-IDF top-100, min_df=2,
max_df=0.8, 1-2-grams; deviations from the original pipeline: no automatic spacing
correction, digits removed by the Korean-only filter). Same 50 stratified 80/20 splits and
fixed CAT specification as the main analysis. FULL fits all preprocessing on the full
sample (mirrors the paper's design); FOLD refits everything within each training partition.

| Overall ΔROC-AUC | −0.00500*** | −0.00531*** |
| Overall ΔPR-AUC | −0.00453*** | −0.00468*** |
| Overall ΔRecall | +0.00182 | +0.00146 |

Text-merged model, FULL − FOLD (paired by seed): ROC +0.00032 (p=0.45), PR-AUC +0.00015
(p=0.78), Recall +0.00036 (p=0.77), F1 +0.00091 (p=0.29) — statistically indistinguishable.

Δ = Text-merged − Structured-only; *** p<0.001, ** p<0.01, * p<0.05 (two-sided paired
